SIM Campus Timetable

Scrape the campus scheduling page, then browse it properly — filter by block, floor and room, or flip to availability mode to find rooms that are actually free.

How it works

  1. Open the SIM scheduling page and log in as usual.
  2. Run the scraper there — paste it into the DevTools console (F12), or use the bookmarklet below.
  3. It walks every page of the table and gives you sim-timetable.json (downloaded, and copied to your clipboard).
  4. Open the viewer and drop that JSON in.

The scraper has to run in your browser because the schedule sits behind your login. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — this site is static, and the viewer parses the JSON locally in your browser.
