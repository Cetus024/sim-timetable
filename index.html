SIM Campus Timetable

Scrape the campus scheduling page, then browse it properly — filter by block, floor and room, or flip to availability mode to find rooms that are actually free.

How it works

  1. Drag the bookmarklet below to your bookmarks bar — once, ever.
  2. Open the SIM scheduling page, log in as usual, and click the bookmarklet.
  3. It opens the viewer, walks every page of the table, and hands the data straight over. No files, no pasting.

Prefer the console? Copy the script and paste it into DevTools (F12) on the scheduling page instead — same result. If the viewer tab gets popup-blocked, the scraper falls back to downloading sim-timetable.json for you to drop into the viewer.

The scraper has to run in your browser because the schedule sits behind your login. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — this site is static, and the data goes straight from the scheduling tab to your viewer tab.
